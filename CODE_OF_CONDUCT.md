# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: showing empathy
and kindness, respecting differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information without permission, and other
conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
project maintainers (see the repository's contact channels, e.g. a private GitHub
security advisory). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter.

This is a summary; the full text and enforcement guidelines are available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
